Added

- The Mécano's controller (spec 12 §2, §6.4, §9, §12): a `TakeCareMecano` reaches `Ready` with its kagent agent and its routes on the gateway once a `cluster` and a `code` connector can be read, and reports `ClusterConnectorMissing` or `CodeConnectorMissing` otherwise; the other slots are blind spots, two Mécanos whose scopes overlap give `ScopeOverlap`, `ScheduleValid` and `nextMaintenanceTime` follow `spec.schedule`, and `Expert` works as `Practitioner`. The agent gets the read tools of its cluster, code, knowledge, changes, history and Map connectors, content tools only when `contentTools` lists them; the `releases` and `copy` connectors get routes for the collector, never tools on the agent. Its maintenances come with a later lot.
- The propose route (spec 12 §6.4): at `Practitioner`, with `maintenance.repositories` set, the route to the `propose` connector carries an argument rule (`<route>-calls`): only `propose_change`, no argument outside `repository`, `branch`, `title`, `body` and `files`, a repository of `maintenance.repositories` that the connector lists, and a branch matching `^takecare/mecano/[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{0,62}$`. It fails closed: the route admits `propose_change`, and the agent is given it, only once the gateway has Accepted and Attached the rule; until then each plan is published without a pull request. A role gets `propose` tools only from its `propose` slot and only at `Practitioner` and above. The Ranger's per-model cap and allowed-models refusal apply to the Mécano's model route.
- The history add-on serves the maintenances (spec 12 §3): `maintenances_list` (state: ids and severities, never text) and `maintenance_get` (content, listed in `addons.history.contentTools`); its Role gains get, list and watch on `takecaremaintenances`.
- `addons.kubernetes.readNodes` and, in the `takecare-cluster` chart, `cluster.readNodes` (spec 12 §4): off by default, on in the demo profile, they grant get, list and watch on `nodes` cluster-wide in a ClusterRole of their own, for the cluster's version; never `nodes/proxy`, `nodes/stats` or `nodes/log`, and the server's `nodes_*` tools stay disabled. The read-only guard now also checks that no connector's ServiceAccount reaches those three subresources.
