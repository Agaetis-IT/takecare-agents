Added

- The Mécano runs its maintenances (spec 12 §3, §9): its weekly schedule creates one, named after the Mécano and the due time, only while the Mécano is Ready and not suspended; a due schedule that meets a running maintenance waits in `pendingTrigger`, and a suspended Mécano, or one whose scope overlaps another's, plans none until it returns. A `TakeCareMaintenance` made by hand runs too; one naming a Mécano that does not exist ends `Failed`. One maintenance runs at a time per Mécano, the others wait `Queued` with `MecanoBusy` or `QueueFull`.
- Each maintenance first runs the collector through the Mécano's routes on the gateway, within half its timeout; the operator's MCP client admits the collector's calls and nothing else (the workload, Certificate, GitOps and removed apiVersion lists of the scope's namespaces, a Certificate by name, the code connector's index, the releases connector's tools). The agent gets the inventory as facts, and its answer is recorded: findings with stable ids, the plan at `Practitioner`, the pull requests opened, the counts and the blind spots, sorted. A finding on a version the inventory did not read is dropped, and a pull request is kept only on a repository of the maintenance and on its branch `takecare/mecano/<maintenance>`.
- Before each `Practitioner` maintenance, the operator checks that the gateway enforces the propose route's rule at its current generation and sends a canary the rule must refuse: without a 403, the message names no repository, the maintenance names the propose connector in its blind spots, and the Mécano gets a `ProposeRuleUnproven` warning event.
- Ended maintenances are kept for `maintenances.retention` (365 days by default); each Mécano keeps its newest ended and its newest published maintenance however old.
