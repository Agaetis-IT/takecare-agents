Added

- The connector type `releases` (spec 12 §7.1), the Mécano's version watch: a connector read by the Mécano's collector, never given to an agent.
- The releases add-on (spec 12 §7.1), `addons.releases`: our server `takecare-agents-mcp-releases` and the connector `releases`, with three read tools over one to thirty-two sources: the tags of an OCI registry, the chart versions of a Helm repository's `index.yaml`, the releases of a GitHub or GitLab project, and the release cycles of endoflife.date products. `versions_list` returns at most 200 full semantic versions (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`, floating tags such as `1.27` left out), newest first, pre-releases flagged, with their dates; `cycles_get` a product's cycles, latest version, maintenance and end-of-life date; `sources_list` each source's host, components, token Secret name and last refresh. It calls only its sources' hosts (and an OCI source's declared token host, `authHost`, for Docker Hub's `auth.docker.io`), refuses a redirect to another host, sends each source's token to that source only, and calls each name at most once a day after an answer; a failed call is retried after 15 minutes, 30 minutes, then at most once an hour, and the last answer is kept, marked stale, for 7 days. A tag list longer than 10 pages of 1,000 tags is a failed call, never a shorter list. A Helm `index.yaml` of at most 16 MiB is streamed, one at a time, and only the asked chart's entries (at most 4 MiB) are decoded. URLs are `https` on port 443 without user information; a `helm` source on `oci://` is refused. A private source's read token is mounted from a Secret on its pod only, sent as basic credentials with a `username`, else as a Bearer token to the source's host only (`PRIVATE-TOKEN` on GitLab); an OCI source whose registry answers with a token realm needs a `username` for its token to be used there. Its ServiceAccount has no Role and no token; its ingress is the gateway and the operator, its egress DNS and TCP 443 only, to `addons.releases.egressCIDRs` (an internal mirror), or the proxy pods, or outside the cluster, the private ranges excluded. Off by default. The release publishes the new image, nine images in all.
